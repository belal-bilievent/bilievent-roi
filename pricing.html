Pricing questions

Before you buy.

How is pricing structured?

Per show, as a monthly subscription for companies with several shows a year, or as a white-label licence for organizers. Improvement delivery is quoted per project. All prices are in SAR and exclude VAT.

Is there a setup fee?

Onboarding for your first show is included in every plan. CRM integration is set up during onboarding.

Can we start with one show?

Yes. Most exhibitors start with a Show ROI package and move to a subscription once they see the first Day 90 report.

What does “we implement the improvements” mean?

After each show the platform recommends what to change: stand layout, staffing, content, activations and follow-up. Bilievent's own events team in Saudi Arabia can then deliver those changes for your next show, and the platform measures them on the same basis.
