FAQ

Questions exhibitors ask us.

What does Bilievent ROI measure?

The full return of an exhibition participation: every cost line (space, build, staff, travel, AV, media), every visitor captured on the stand, the leads that qualified, the opportunities and deals they became in your CRM, and the resulting cost per qualified lead and ROI per show.

How does the AI qualify leads?

Each lead is scored on fit and intent: the visitor's role and authority, budget signals, timeline, company fit and engagement on the stand. The score always shows its reasons, and the model is retrained on which leads actually became customers at your past shows.

Does it work at any exhibition in Saudi Arabia?

Yes. Your team captures visitors with badge scan, QR code, business card, tablet form or a voice note, so it does not depend on one organizer's app. It also works offline on the show floor and syncs when the signal returns.

Can my team speak in Arabic?

Yes. Salespeople can record voice notes in Arabic or English; the platform turns them into structured notes on the lead record.

Which CRMs do you connect to?

We connect to common CRMs and can export to any system. During onboarding we map your stages so that opportunities and won deals are traced back to the show they came from.

What does “we implement the improvements” mean?

After each show the platform recommends what to change: stand layout, staffing, content, activations and follow-up. Bilievent's own events team in Saudi Arabia can then deliver those changes for your next show, and the platform measures them on the same basis.

How is pricing structured?

Per show, as a monthly subscription for companies with several shows a year, or as a white-label licence for organizers. Improvement delivery is quoted per project. All prices are in SAR and exclude VAT.

What is in the annual programme?

For each show in your calendar: a target list prepared 8 weeks before, an executive roundtable for up to 40 target companies, a speaker slot for your spokesperson, a signature booth reused at every show with smart cameras and staff headsets, media coverage and personal branding, AI visibility, a 72-hour follow-up after the show, lead filtering, and ROI measured for the whole year.

The screens on this site are a design preview with sample data. Book a demo to see the platform on your own exhibition.
